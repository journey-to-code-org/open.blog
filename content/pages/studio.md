Studio is the editor that comes with open.blog. It runs in a browser, on a computer or a
phone, and it writes the same Markdown files you could edit by hand. Nothing is kept anywhere
else, so you can stop using it at any time and lose nothing.

```bash
openblog user add you
openblog serve --admin
```

Then open `/admin` and sign in. [Studio: running your blog](https://journey-to-code-org.github.io/open.blog/docs/admin/) is the full guide.

## Home

Home asks one question, "What would you like to do?", and lists what is waiting: drafts to
keep going with, posts coming up, and what was published last.

![Studio's Home screen: Write something new at the top, then a draft, a scheduled post and the three newest published posts.](/images/studio/home.jpg)

## Writing

A post opens with fields for what its kind needs: a recipe has servings and times above its
words. **See how it looks** shows the page as readers will get it, and the button beside it
puts it on the site.

![Studio's writing screen open on a recipe, with Serves, Time to get ready and Time to cook above the recipe's words.](/images/studio/write-recipe.jpg)

[Writing in Studio](/posts/writing-in-studio/) covers the editor, and
[Posting a family recipe without a terminal](/posts/posting-a-family-recipe-without-a-terminal/)
walks through one from start to finish.

## Every post in one list

**Posts** shows drafts, scheduled posts and published ones, with a box to find one by name.

![Studio's Posts screen: counts of drafts, scheduled and published posts, and the list with each post's date and state.](/images/studio/posts.jpg)

## The front page

**Front page** is the home page as a list of cards. Add a section, change it, move it, hide
it, and see the result before saving. Earlier versions are kept.

![Studio's Front page screen: this site's home page as cards for the welcome, the buttons, text, a picture and the lists.](/images/studio/front-page.jpg)

## Pages that are not posts

**Pages** lists the pages that stay put, such as About, and **Collections** the series that
group posts. Each opens in the same editor as a post.

![Studio's Pages screen, listing this site's six pages with Add a page above them.](/images/studio/pages.jpg)

## The look

**Look** starts from a ready-made look, then changes the main color, the lettering, the size
of the writing, the corners and the spacing, for every page. **Decorations** puts a different
look up for a season, by the calendar or today.

![Studio's Look screen: a row of ready-made looks, then the main color and the font style.](/images/studio/look.jpg)

[Making it look like yours](/posts/making-it-look-like-yours/) has the details.

## Readers

**Readers** has three parts. **Visitors** counts who has been reading over the last week,
month, season or year. **What readers sent** holds comments until you choose what to do with
them. **How readers reach you** is where they are set up. Nothing about a reader is stored
beyond a count.

## On a phone

Every screen is one column on a phone, with the same buttons.

![Studio's Home screen on a phone.](/images/studio/home-phone.jpg)

## What else is there

- **Pictures**, for everything you have uploaded.
- **Services**, **Reviews**, **Business** and **New job**, when the site is for a trade.
- **Research**, for sources, notes and a notebook, when you write from sources.
- A list of everything Studio can do, opened with `Ctrl+K`.
- **Getting started**, which walks a new owner through the first sign-in.
- An optional writing helper. It is off until you turn it on, and it says what it sends before it sends it.
