This is the demo blog for [open.blog](https://github.com/journey-to-code-org/open.blog), a
blogging engine that turns a folder of Markdown files into a fast, static website. It comes with
a browser editor for people who would rather not touch the files.

Every post here is a working example of a feature, with the instructions to use it on your own
site. [Welcome to your blog](/posts/welcome-to-your-blog/) is the map.

## What makes it different

- **Your words stay yours.** Posts are plain text files in a folder you own. There is no
  database to back up and nothing to export.
- **Readers are not tracked.** View counts, comments and share links are built in, with no
  cookies and no third-party scripts.
- **It is not only for code.** Recipes, trade jobs, field journals, research papers and video
  pages each get the features they need.
- **It works everywhere.** Any static host can serve the site. Feeds, search and interactive
  lessons all degrade gracefully when JavaScript is off.

## Make this page yours

This page is `content/pages/about.md`. Pages work like posts without dates, tags or series, and
live at `/<file name>/`. `nav: true` in the front matter puts this one in the header.

Replace this text with something about you: who you are, what you write about, and how readers
can reach you. A [contact piece](/posts/buttons-maps-hours-and-faqs/#contact-details) works well
here.
