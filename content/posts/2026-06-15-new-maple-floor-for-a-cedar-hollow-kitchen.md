## Problem

The kitchen had two layers of vinyl tile, the top one cracked along the sink run. The owners
wanted wood that would continue into the dining room, which had worn carpet, without a step or
a threshold strip between the rooms.

## What we found

Under the vinyl, the plywood subfloor dipped 6 mm over two metres beside the old dishwasher,
where a slow leak had softened it years ago. It was dry now but uneven. The dining room
subfloor was flat.

## What we did

1. Removed both layers of vinyl and the dining room carpet.
2. Cut out and replaced the soft plywood, screwed the whole subfloor down, and levelled the dip
   with self-levelling compound.
3. Left the maple in the house for seven days to adjust to the room's humidity.
4. Laid the boards in one continuous run through both rooms, nailed every 20 cm.
5. Sanded flush and applied three coats of satin finish.

## Result

One floor through both rooms, with no seam and no step. The owners chose satin over matte
because it hides less dirt, which is a good trade in a kitchen with two dogs.
