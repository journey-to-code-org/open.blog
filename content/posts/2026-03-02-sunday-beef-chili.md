This is the chili for a cold Sunday: twenty minutes of chopping, then a long, lazy simmer that
makes the house smell wonderful. It is better the next day, so it is worth making a full pot.

## What you need

- 2 lb / 900 g ground beef (15% fat)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 large onions, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 28 oz / 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans kidney beans (15 oz / 425 g each), drained and rinsed
- 1 cup (240 ml) beef stock
- 1 square (10 g) dark chocolate
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Sour cream, cheddar and sliced green onions, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in two batches,
   about 5 minutes each, then lift it out with a slotted spoon.
2. Lower the heat to medium. Cook the onions and pepper in the fat left in the pot until soft,
   8-10 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, spices and tomato paste and stir for 2 minutes, until dark and fragrant.
4. Return the beef with the tomatoes, beans, stock and chocolate. Bring to a boil.
5. Turn the heat to low, partly cover, and simmer for 1 hr 30 mins, stirring now and then.
6. Season with salt and pepper. If it is too thin, simmer uncovered for 10 minutes more.
7. Serve with sour cream, cheddar and green onions.
