Browning the butter takes five extra minutes and makes these taste like toffee. The short chill
keeps them thick instead of spreading into one giant cookie.

This recipe shows three more recipe features:

- **A batch, not servings.** `makes: 24 cookies` is shown on the card, and `servings: 24` lets
  readers scale the batch. Set the scaler to 12 for a dozen, or 48 for a bake sale.
- **Two measures at once.** `1 cup (225 g) butter` scales both numbers together.
- **Ingredient groups.** `### For the dough` and `### To finish` are sub-headings inside the
  ingredients, and each group still gets its checkboxes.

> [!TIP]
> Weigh the flour if you can. A cup of flour can weigh anything from 120 g to 160 g depending on
> how it is scooped, and too much makes a dry, cakey cookie.

## Ingredients

### For the dough

- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter
- 1 cup (200 g) packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100 g) white sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 1 1/2 cups (255 g) dark chocolate chips or chopped chocolate

### To finish

- Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling

## Steps

1. Melt the butter in a light-coloured pan over medium heat. Keep swirling until it foams, then
   turns golden with brown specks and smells nutty, about 5-7 minutes. Pour it into a large bowl
   with every brown bit and cool for 10 minutes.
2. Whisk both sugars into the butter, then the eggs and vanilla, until glossy, about 1 minute.
3. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt until just combined, then fold in the chocolate.
4. Cover the bowl and chill the dough for 30 minutes. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 350°F (175°C)
   and line two trays with baking paper.
5. Roll the dough into 24 balls and space them well apart on the trays.
6. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are set and golden but the middles look underdone.
7. Sprinkle with flaky salt and leave on the tray for 5 minutes before moving them to a rack.

## Notes

Unbaked dough balls freeze for three months. Bake them from frozen and add 2 minutes.
