Every part of this series is a working example. You see the feature on the page, and the
Markdown that made it sits right below it. Copy any block into your own post and it works the
same way.

You do not need to remember any of the syntax. In the Admin editor, **Add something** writes
buttons, maps, opening hours, FAQs and before-and-after pairs for you, and **Add knowledge check**
does the same for quizzes. The last part shows how.
