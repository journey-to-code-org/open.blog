Start here. These four parts cover what a new blog needs: what a post file holds, how tags and
series organise posts, how building, drafts and scheduling work, and how to cross-post without
losing credit for your writing.

This series uses the `demo` theme, so its pages look different from the rest of the site. That is
per-series theming, and it takes one line (`theme: demo`) in `content/series/getting-started.md`.
