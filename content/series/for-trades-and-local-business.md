A tradesperson's best advertisement is their finished work. These parts follow **Maple Falls
Floors**, an invented flooring business, through the job and service features: a guide to set
them up, then two real-looking job write-ups with before-and-after photos.

Because this demo site has services and jobs, the header gained a **Services** link and the site
gained [/services/](/services/) and [/jobs/](/jobs/) pages, all without any configuration.
