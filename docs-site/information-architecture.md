## Design goals

- **Task-oriented**: readers can move from “what is this” to “how do I do X”.
- **Progressive detail**: overviews first, references second, operations last.
- **Operator-ready**: deployment and troubleshooting are first-class docs.
- **Author-friendly**: Markdown and front matter guidance are explicit and reusable.
