# ONB1: Grammi walks it (recipes, phone, 400 px)

By `user-grammi`, 2026-11-16. Walked twice: first against `ux.md` (before building), then on the
running Studio at 400 px (screenshots of each screen, and the browser journey in
`tests/browser/studio-onboarding.spec.mjs`, "Grammi on a phone"). The persona was played by the
agent that built the slice, following `.claude/agents/user-grammi.md`; it speaks only from what
she sees.

## The walk

1. I sign in with the password my son gave me. It says **Welcome** and "A few questions so your
   blog shows you what you need. Skip any of them." That's friendly. "Question 1 of 6", so I know
   it won't take long.
2. "What will you mostly write?" Big boxes. I tap **Recipes and family stories**. Nothing jumps
   ahead on me, so I can look before I press **Next**. Good: I tap things by accident.
3. "Will anyone else write with you?" **Just me**. "Should your blog dress up for holidays and
   seasons?" Well, yes! **Yes** says "Decorations, greeting cards and gifts." That's what I want.
4. "Do you want to share posts beyond your blog?" I'm not sure what that means for me, so **Maybe
   later**. Comments: **Yes**. "How much would you like to see?" I tap **Skip this one**.
5. "Here is what your blog will show you." It lists Recipes ("Because you write recipes and family
   stories"), Seasons and holidays, Pages ("Already on your blog"). Under **Tucked away** it says
   Sharing, "You said maybe later." It tells me they are one step away. I press **Save these** and
   it says "Saved. Your blog now shows what you need."
6. "Want your blog set up to match? Nothing changes unless you say so." One card: "Choose
   decorations for the seasons" with **Open Decorations**. I'll do that later. **Next**.
7. "Want your blog to learn how you write? Three short questions, three minutes each... One of them
   is always a true story from your life." I press **Start**.
8. It goes to telling stories. I choose "A mistake I made", **Type it**, and write about the year I
   forgot the rolls. **I'm done**, then **That's enough for now**. The helper part I skip with
   **Not now**. "You're ready."
9. On Home the menu has Decorations and no Sharing. Later I write a new recipe, open **More**, and
   **Stories you could tell** shows my rolls story right there beside it. It says it isn't put in my
   post, so I copy the bit I want.

## Stuck

None.

## Unsure

- **"Collections"** said "Posts gathered into a series." I didn't know what a series was.
  *Folded back:* now "Posts you group together, like a series." (`area.collectionsAbout`); the name
  stays Collections because that is what the menu already calls it.
- **"Writing together · You didn't say, so it stays."** when I skipped that question: I wasn't sure
  if I'd done something wrong. *Folded back:* kept; the words say nothing is wrong, and **Change**
  sits beside it.
- On the summary the **Change** links were blue and far apart. *Folded back:* they now sit at the
  end of each line in the blog's colour, still big enough to tap.

## Liked

- One question at a time, big boxes, and nothing moves on until I press Next.
- It told me why each thing is shown, in my words.
- "Nothing changes unless you say so."
- My story was there when I sat down to write a recipe, and it didn't put itself in my post.
