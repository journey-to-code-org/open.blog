# Grammi walks through Studio, slice 1 (from the design, on my phone)
Read from design.md sections 2-4 against brief check 1. All of it is me, Grammi.

## How it went

I tapped the bookmark Mikey made. It says "Grammi's House", which is mine, good. "Your name": is
that "Grammi" or my real name? The picture shows "grammi" in small letters, so I guess that. If I
forget my password there is nothing to tap, so I'd call Mikey. Signed in fine.

Home asks "What would you like to do?" and there's one big button, **Write something new**. Easy.
Title first: "Add a title and your draft will save itself." I typed Pumpkin bread and it said
"saved just now". That's nice. Then "Words". I wrote two paragraphs, pressing Enter once between
them like I do in an email. Nothing tells me if that makes two paragraphs on my site or one big
lump. I wouldn't know until I looked.

"When it appears", Date and Time, "Empty: the day you publish". I left it alone. Fine.

Tags: I typed Thanksgiving. Then what? The computer picture has a [+], my phone doesn't. Enter? A
comma? And the little x is tiny; my thumb would take the tag off.

Cover photo: **Choose from your pictures** sounds like MY pictures on my phone, but it's the site's.
The other says "Drop a photo or choose". I can't drop anything on a phone. I'd try both. The
progress bar is good, and the pumpkin bread example for describing the picture, I get it.

**Save draft**: "Saved as a draft." Good. Later, Home, "Keep going", there's my pumpkin bread with
its picture. Lovely. **Publish**: "Your readers will see it in about a minute." I want to look at
it, but "View the live post" is hidden in More, or under the little "G". I wouldn't find it.

Posts: Menu, then Posts, then "Find a post", type pump. There it is, "Published". Fixed my word.
It says "changes not on your site yet". Now I have **Save draft** and **Update**. Will Save draft
take my recipe off the site and make it a draft again? That scares me. I'd press Update and hope.

Closing the page halfway: the phone pops up "Leave site?" and that sounds like I'm losing
everything. Next time, "You have changes to this post that aren't on your site yet" with **Put my
changes back**. That I understand. But if I start on my phone and open it on the computer, are my
changes there? It says "on this device", so I think not.

"Changed somewhere else": who? Mikey, or did someone get in? **See the newer version** takes my
words off the screen and gives me "Copy all"; copy and paste on a phone is beyond me. **Keep mine
instead** "replaces the other changes", and I won't wipe out Mikey's work. I'd call him.

## Stuck
- The "changed somewhere else" message: I can't tell who or what changed, and both choices sound
  like I'll ruin somebody's work. Copy and paste on the phone is not something I do.
- Forgot my password: no way back in without Mikey.
- Finding my phone's photos: "Choose from your pictures" isn't my pictures, and "Drop" means
  nothing on a phone.

## Unsure
- Two paragraphs: does one Enter make a new paragraph on the site? Nothing shows me.
- How to finish a tag on the phone, and the tiny x that takes it off.
- On a published post, does **Save draft** take it down? Which button do I press?
- After Publish, where do I see it on my site? It's tucked in More.
- "Kept on this device": will my computer have the changes I made on my phone?
- "Your name": which name? (lowercase "grammi"?)
- The phone's "Leave site?" box sounds like I lose everything.

## Liked
- It says "Grammi's House" at the top. It's my house.
- "What would you like to do?" and one big button.
- "Your draft will save itself" and "saved just now".
- "Keep going" with my recipe's picture and "Changed today at 3 PM".
- "Your readers will see it in about a minute." Tells me when, plainly.
- "Put my changes back" / "Use what's on the site". Plain choices.
